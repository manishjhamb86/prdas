About Pranabesh

Experience built on projects, people and principles.

Pranabesh R Das brings more than four decades of leadership in engineering, procurement, construction and project management, with extensive experience in oil and gas, refineries and petrochemicals.

His work spans grassroots project conception through commissioning, drawing on leadership roles at Indian Oil Corporation and major international EPC organisations. He is particularly focused on fast-track delivery, modular construction, accountability and zero-accident safety management.
